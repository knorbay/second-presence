# Changes

## 0.9 — 11 October 2026

- Day two builds tension through electrical faults and ventilation noises without an active hunter. Day three includes a 65-second opening with two warnings; the hunter enters out of sight and away from the player. Later shifts have a short opening grace period.
- Old day-two saves cannot restore an early chase. Loading does not replay earlier ambience cues.
- Wiring, valves, filter cleaning, cargo and confirmation/error actions have distinct Foley. Idle panels no longer repeat metal impacts. Creature sounds use low breath and scraping instead of the previous slime effect.
- Ventilation and machinery loops have softened seams, sparse regional details and positional attenuation. Decoys and creature cues respect distance and walls.
- English and Turkish wording reviewed across daily briefings, panels, crew logs and feedback. Fixed late catalogue entries missing from composite labels; dynamic death and shift summaries use complete sentence translations.
- Daily briefing shows both the incident plan and the current ship conditions.
- Six incident plans, five jobs with varying stages and dependencies, and seeded puzzle targets. New attempts change the plan; loading preserves it.
- Asymmetric creature silhouette, frontal capture animation and irregular movement cadence.

Version remains 0.9; the public release uses the provisional Second Presence name.
