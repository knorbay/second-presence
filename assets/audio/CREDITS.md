- Airflow, distant strain and breath layers are generated locally by the game.

Sci-fi/ambience sources were checked on 2026-10-07; Impact Sounds on 2026-10-08. These are game sound assets; none are claimed to be recordings from a real spacecraft. The final mix uses gradual room-dependent changes and dedicated footstep channels so ambient/effect traffic cannot suppress contacts.

The October 11 mix adds dry, filtered layers for switches, valves, filter brushing,
cargo contacts, ventilation knocks/dragging, and confirmation/error feedback.
These are derived from the same bundled CC0 Kenney contacts and locally generated
breath/noise. The creature voice no longer uses slime_000. No new third-party audio
was added. The original files remain unmodified; layers are built at startup.
